# Backlink Playbook (Malaysia Focus)

## Goal
Acquire high-relevance backlinks that strengthen ranking for:
- `media buying services Malaysia`
- `strategic communications and PR Malaysia`
- `media intelligence services Malaysia`
- `event management services Malaysia`

## Link Targets We Built
- Home: `https://aacwltech.net/`
- Media Buying: `https://aacwltech.net/media-buying-malaysia.html`
- Strategic Communications PR: `https://aacwltech.net/strategic-communications-pr-malaysia.html`
- Media Intelligence: `https://aacwltech.net/media-intelligence-malaysia.html`
- Event Management: `https://aacwltech.net/event-management-malaysia.html`
- Portfolio: `https://aacwltech.net/work.html`

## Weekly Workflow
1. Open `seo/backlink-tracker.csv`.
2. Prioritize 5 targets with `status=Pending`.
3. Send outreach using `seo/outreach-templates.md`.
4. Update `status` to `Contacted`.
5. Follow up in 5-7 days.
6. Update to `Live` once backlink is indexed and visible.

## Anchor Text Rules
- Keep anchors natural and readable.
- Do not repeat one exact keyword too many times.
- Mix brand and service anchors:
  - `AACWL TECH SDN BHD`
  - `media buying services Malaysia`
  - `strategic communications and PR Malaysia`
  - `campaign execution portfolio`

## Quality Rules
- Prioritize websites that are relevant to media, communications, business or public programmes.
- Avoid spammy directories with thin content.
- Prefer backlinks on indexed pages that have real visitors.
- Keep outreach factual: no exaggerated claims.
